# messing-around-with-python
I just started learning python, here is something i made trying to incorporate the basics into something I found a bit of fun, whilst also teaching me how to use it.
it starts by asking your name, then greets you and asks your age. it tells you what year you were born in, and then asks you how many levels you want to be made. it creates a pyramid(or attempts to). it then translates whatever you said into morse. from there, it quizzes you on trivia, gives you a score, then leaves.
